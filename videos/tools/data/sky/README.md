Star and constellation data from d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn (https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial, BSD 3-clause,
see LICENSE-d3-celestial): stars to magnitude 6 (Yale BSC/Hipparcos, mag, B–V), constellation stick figures, label
positions and names, IAU constellation boundaries, Milky Way outlines. Coordinates: [RA in degrees (−180…180), Dec].

Photographic sky: `eso0932a.jpg` — «The Milky Way panorama», ESO/S. Brunier (CC BY 4.0), 6000×3000, galactic
coordinates (the Galactic Centre in the middle, longitude growing to the left). Credit it in the video.
