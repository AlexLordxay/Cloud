Mars has a mountain two and a half times taller than Everest.

Meet Mars. The fourth planet from the Sun, half the size of Earth.

Olympus Mons — twenty-two kilometers high. It's so wide that its base hides beyond the horizon.

And this is Valles Marineris. Four thousand kilometers long — almost as long as the United States is wide.

Mars is red because its dust is ordinary rust — iron oxide, like on an old nail.

Mars has two moons, Phobos and Deimos. Phobos is slowly falling toward the planet: one day it will be torn apart into a ring.

Here you'd weigh almost three times less. And a sunset on Mars is blue.
