Are you an Aries? Then your constellation once opened the astronomical year: two thousand years ago, on the spring equinox, the Sun stood right in Aries.

Today the Sun passes through Aries from April nineteenth to May fourteenth.

Its brightest star is the orange giant Hamal. And Aries hides a faint red star, Teegarden's Star, just twelve light-years away. Planets circle it — and some of them might even have water.

By the way, if you're an Aries by your horoscope, on your birthday the Sun was most likely in Pisces. It was in Aries two thousand years ago.
