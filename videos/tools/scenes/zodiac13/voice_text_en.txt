There aren't twelve signs of the zodiac. There are thirteen.

Over a year, the Sun passes through constellations in our sky — that's the zodiac.

But in reality, it crosses thirteen constellations. The thirteenth is Ophiuchus.

From November twenty-ninth to December seventeenth, the Sun is right inside it. And in Scorpius — only for a week.

So why isn't it in horoscopes? More than two and a half thousand years ago, the Babylonians split the sky into twelve equal parts — it was easier to count.

Since then, Earth's axis has turned, and all the signs have shifted by almost a whole month.

By the way, in sixteen-oh-four a supernova exploded in Ophiuchus — the last exploding star in our galaxy that people saw with the naked eye.
