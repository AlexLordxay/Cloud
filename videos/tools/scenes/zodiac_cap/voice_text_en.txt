Are you a Capricorn? Then your constellation is one of the oldest: a sea-goat with a fish's tail was drawn as far back as Babylon.

Earth's southern tropic is still called the Tropic of Capricorn — because two thousand years ago, on the shortest day of the year, the Sun stood right here.

The Sun passes through Capricornus from January nineteenth to February sixteenth.

Look closely at its horn: two stars that look like a pair to the naked eye. In fact, they're nowhere near each other — they just happen to line up.

By the way, if you're a Capricorn by your horoscope, on your birthday the Sun was actually in Sagittarius — close to the center of our galaxy.
