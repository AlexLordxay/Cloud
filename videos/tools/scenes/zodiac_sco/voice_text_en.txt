Are you a Scorpio? Then your constellation is the shortest in the zodiac: the Sun crosses it in just one week, from November twenty-third to the twenty-ninth.

At its heart is Antares. The name means "rival of Mars," because it's just as red.

It's a red supergiant. Put it in place of the Sun, and it would swallow Mercury, Venus, Earth — and even Mars.

Antares is aging, and one day it will explode as a supernova — bright enough to see even in daylight.

From the northern half of the world, Scorpius sits low in the south on summer evenings.

By the way, on your birthday you can't see Scorpius — it hides behind the Sun. You'll see your sign best half a year later, in June.
