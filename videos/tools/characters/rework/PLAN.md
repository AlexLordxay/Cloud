## План (архітектор)

**Сумісність:** These must keep working in all three steps (blackhole, survive and astrodance use them):

- **Joints.** All 15 names stay: body, head, shoulderL/R, elbowL/R, wristL/R, hipL/R, kneeL/R, ankleL/R. They become THREE.Bone, which is still an Object3D.
  - Euler order stays XYZ, and every sign convention stays: elbow +x flexes the forearm to -z; knee -x flexes the shin to +z; hip +x flexes forward; shoulder z = s*abduction (left s = -1 at -x); wrist +x flexes the palm forward; ankle -x points the toes down.

Scenes also set group position, rotation and scale (including the 'YXZ' order in blackhole); the character never writes group transforms.
- **pose(t).** It stays a pure, stateless function of t:
  - blackhole calls pose(5.5k+0.6) on a slowing clock;
  - survive jumps t between segments;
  - astrodance calls pose(t*0.5) and relies on it for wrists and ankles.
  - It resets every joint it touches on every call, including all finger bones. It never writes body.position or group transforms.
  - Scenes override joints after pose(t). So all skin-only logic (helper slerps, morph influences) runs inside the helper bones' updateMatrix during updateMatrixWorld, never inside pose(t).
- **Grounding.** survive's placeOnGround uses Box3.setFromObject(group), and in r147 that ignores skinning. Never set vertexColors on a material shared by meshes that lack a color attribute. Do not change THREE.ColorManagement.legacyMode.
- **Skinned meshes.**

  - Set frustumCulled = false. Keep within 4 influences per vertex, and don't let weight bands overlap. Set morphAttributes before constructing the mesh, with morphTargetsRelative = true.

- **Visor.** If Step 3 moves the visor outward, survive/cam.js shell() must move its overlay radius and centre to a.visor (r+0.0025) in the same change, or the crack and frost disappear.
- **Performance.** swiftshader renders take 10-30 s, so keep the total vertex count moderate (about 150k or less) and keep the 2 shadow-casting lights as they are.
